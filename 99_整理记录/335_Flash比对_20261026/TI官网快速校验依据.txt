<p><strong>Verification Options:</strong> After the code/data is loaded to the device, sets the level of verification performed on it. This is done by reading back the code/data from the device's memory and comparing it to the executable on the host. The choices are:</p>
<ul class="simple">
<li><p><strong>Full verification:</strong> Reads back the entire program loaded to the device.</p></li>
<li><p><strong>Fast verification:</strong> Reads back only the first and last 20 words loaded to the device. This is the default option for most devices.</p></li>
<li><p><strong>No verification:</strong> No verification is performed.</p></li>
</ul>
